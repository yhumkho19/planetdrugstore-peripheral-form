      Employee Daily Tasks

      NEW UPDATES!

      This announcement appears when you enter Daily Tasks from Employee Home, not on refresh. Older announcements are saved under Update history at the bottom-right of Employee Home.

      • 1
        Update summary removed

        The side summary is gone, giving you more room to fill in the task. Required-field reminders remain below the form.

      • 2
        Floating “Submitting as” panel

        Drag the employee details and Pending logs panel anywhere on the screen. Its position is remembered in this browser.

      • 3
        Hide or collapse the panel

        Collapse it to save space or hide it and bring it back with the “Submitting as” button.

      • 4
        Refresh without interruptions

        This notice opens when you enter from Employee Home. Refreshing the Daily Tasks page will not open it again; return Home and enter Daily Tasks again to see it, unless you choose “Hide for 7 days.”

      • 5
        Older announcements stay available

        Open “Update history” at the bottom-right of Employee Home to review previous Billing Daily Tasks updates.

      • 6
        Billing Home navigation fixed

        Billing Head now returns to the Billing landing page from Daily Tasks and Billing Schedule instead of opening the dashboard.

      • 7
        No Status tasks stay visible

        If you submit without choosing a status, the task appears in Pending Logs with “No status” so you can update it later.

      • 8
        Confirm before clearing the form

        Click Clear and confirm to remove all filled fields and attached photos. Choose Cancel or close the confirmation to keep everything as entered.

      • 9
        Billing Head task messages

        In My History, the icon shows whether the latest update is a message or a reply. Its unread number disappears after you open the conversation; messages and replies also appear in your notification bell.

      • 10
        Task messages and private chat have separate tabs

        Open Messages to switch between comments or replies about your tasks and your private chat with Billing Head. Each entry opens its matching conversation.

      • 11
        See when a task reaction changes

        If Billing Head replaces a reaction, your notification shows both the old and new reactions, such as “Heart → Wow.” First-time reactions are identified separately.

      • 12
        Profile photo in Messages

        Task message and private chat entries show Billing Head’s profile photo when one is available.

      • 13
        Mobile actions are grouped in a menu

        On a phone, tap the three-line menu above “Billing Department” to open Go back home, Log an update, My history, Messages, and Notifications.
