Billing Daily Tasks

NEW UPDATES!

This announcement appears when you enter Daily Tasks from Billing Home, not on refresh. Older announcements are saved under Update history at the bottom-right of Billing Home.

  • 1
    Shows when you enter from Billing Home

    The NEW UPDATES notice opens when you choose Billing Daily Task Tracker from Billing Home. Refreshing the Daily Tasks page will not open it again.

  • 2
    Return to see it again

    Go back to Billing Home and open Daily Tasks again to see this announcement, unless you choose “Hide for 7 days.”

  • 3
    Older announcements stay available

    Open “Update history” at the bottom-right of Billing Home to review previous Billing Daily Tasks updates.

  • 4
    Billing Home navigation fixed

    Use “Billing Home” from Daily Tasks or Billing Schedule to return to the Billing landing page; it will no longer open the dashboard.

  • 5
    No Status tasks are tracked

    The task meter now counts submissions without a status, and the “No Status” filter shows them separately.

  • 6
    Updated filters and task details

    Click “Filter tasks” to hide or show the filters. Use the right arrow (>) to show the status filters and the left arrow (<) to hide them. Each submission starts collapsed to show only the employee name, department, and status; use its down arrow to show details and up arrow to hide them with an animation.

  • 7
    Custom confirmation for deleting all data

    Click “Delete all data” to open the confirmation modal. Type DELETE exactly and confirm to permanently remove all daily tasks, edit history, and messages; cancel to keep the data.

  • 8
    Messages and employee replies

    Send a message from Messages whenever you need to contact the employee. The employee can reply to each message, and you can continue each conversation after they respond. The Replies button shows unread employee replies and clears its count when opened.

  • 9
    Task messages and private chats in one inbox

    Open Messages and use the Task messages and Private chat tabs to switch between employee task conversations and private chats. The two message types stay separate.

  • 10
    All Billing employees are in Private chat

    Choose an employee from the Private chat tab to start or continue a conversation. The separate Billing employees button and list have been removed.

  • 11
    Profile photos and reaction-change alerts

    Message entries show profile photos when available. If you change a task reaction, the employee is notified which reaction changed, such as “Heart → Wow.”
