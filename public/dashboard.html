About the portal

Daily work, brought into focus.

PLANETDRUGSTORE brings event staffing, employee schedules, stock requests, and peripherals receiving records into a shared operations experience. Teams can find the right workflow, act on current information, and keep important updates visible.

Employees and administrators have separate sign-in routes, with access directed to the pages used for their role.

Frequently asked

A few quick answers.

Need more help? Contact your department head or IT/Admin through your usual Planet Drugstore channel.

Which sign-in should I use?

Employees should use Employee Sign In with their Staff ID. Department heads and administrators should use Admin Sign In with their authorized email account.

Where can I find schedules and event assignments?

Sign in as an employee to open your schedule and review published event assignments, open roles, and schedule updates.

How do I submit a stock request?

Sign in with your employee account. Stock Request access depends on your department, assigned request days, and whether the request window is open.

Can I access the portal on mobile?

Yes. The web portal supports mobile browsers, and schedule notifications may also be available through the Android companion app.
